# Amazon-Sales-Analysis
Analyzed Amazon sales data using SQL, Advanced Excel, and Power BI to identify sales trends, product performance, and key business insights through interactive dashboards.


## Objective
Analyze Amazon sales data to identify sales trends, product and category performance, order patterns, and key business insights. Build an interactive Power BI dashboard to present the analysis in an easy-to-understand visual format.


### Skills Learned

°Proficiency in SQL for data querying, filtering, and aggregation.
•Advanced Excel skills for data analysis, calculations, and reporting.
•Proficiency in Power BI for creating interactive dashboards and KPI reports.
•Understanding of sales trends, product performance, and business insights.
•Ability to transform raw data into actionable business insights.

### Tools Used

•SQL: Querying, filtering, aggregating, and analyzing sales data.
•Advanced Excel: Data preparation, calculations, analysis, and reporting.
•Power BI: Creating interactive dashboards, KPI visualizations, and business reports.

## Steps

Below are the key steps taken in the VAPT process:

### 1. **Data Collection & Understanding**

Collected and reviewed the Amazon sales dataset to understand the available fields, including product details, sales, quantity, customer information, payment methods, and order status.

### 2. **Data Cleaning & Preparation**

Cleaned and prepared the dataset by checking data quality, handling inconsistencies, organizing columns, and preparing the data for analysis.

###3. **Exploratory Data Analysis**

Used Advanced Excel to explore the dataset, identify patterns, analyze sales performance, and understand product and category trends.

###4. **SQL Analysis**

Used SQL queries to filter, aggregate, and analyze sales data to extract meaningful information about sales performance, order trends, and product-level insights.

###5. **Power BI Dashboard Development**

Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to visualize key performance indicators (KPIs), sales trends, category performance, and product-level results.

###6. **Business Insights**

Analyzed the results to identify important sales trends, top-performing products, category performance, and insights that support data-driven business decisions
